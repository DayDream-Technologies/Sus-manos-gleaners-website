Being His Hands to Feed a Hungry World

About Sus Manos Gleaners

Rescuing food. Nourishing lives. Serving the world.

Sus Manos Gleaners is a faith-based nonprofit organization in Jenison, Michigan, dedicated to transforming surplus produce from West Michigan farms into nutritious, shelf-stable food for people facing hunger around the world.

Our story began in 2004, when Jim and Tim Paauw traveled with a church youth group to British Columbia, Canada. While volunteering with a food gleaning ministry, they witnessed how donated produce and willing hands could provide thousands of meals to people in need.

Inspired by that experience, they founded Sus Manos Gleaners in 2008.

The name Sus Manos, Spanish for “His Hands,” reflects our belief that we are called to put our faith into action by serving others.

Today, we bring together local farmers, volunteers, businesses, churches, and supporters to rescue surplus fruits and vegetables that might otherwise go to waste. Volunteers wash, prepare, and dehydrate this produce, creating nutritious food that can be safely stored and efficiently shipped through trusted Christian ministry partners to communities experiencing hunger.

What began as a small volunteer effort has grown into a global mission. As of 2026, Sus Manos has shipped over 14 million servings of food across 19 countries, reaching children, families, and communities affected by poverty, food insecurity, and humanitarian crises.

Yet our mission is about more than food.

It is about bringing a community together in service. It is about stewarding the abundance of our local farms. It is about recognizing the dignity and worth of every person and responding to hunger with compassion and generosity.

And we believe there is still much more to accomplish.

As demand and opportunities continue to grow, we are working toward expanding our facilities and capacity so we can welcome more volunteers, rescue more produce, and provide more nutritious food to families around the world.

At Sus Manos Gleaners, we believe that when many hands come together in service, we can be His Hands to a hungry world.

We invite you to be part of that story.
