What We Do

The gleaning and dehydration process

  1. Step 1

    Rescuing surplus harvest

    Direct from farmers. Rescuing bulk field surpluses directly before they go to waste.

    Direct from grocers. Lakeshore Food Rescue and pantry partnerships intercept excess or near-date grocery and pantry inventory before it spoils.

  2. Step 2

    Volunteer preparation

    Multi-generational volunteers clean, trim, slice, and prepare produce across our Jenison warehouse prep tables.

    That line is our processing power. A grower cannot manually open 5,000 retail-packaged bags of surplus produce to repurpose them. Our volunteers, including groups as young as 2nd graders, unpack that produce and load it onto the conveyor in a single shift.

  3. Step 3

    Industrial dehydration

    Produce is lined across food-grade mesh trays and dried in commercial dehydrators, maximizing shelf life and nutrient density.

  4. Step 4

    Bagging, packing, and shipping

    Dried vegetables are bagged, packed into boxes, palletized, and coordinated for international shipping, in Christ's name.
